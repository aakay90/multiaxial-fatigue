# Wang–Sun–Liu–Liu–Shang 2022 (`WANG2022`)

> **Registry name:** `WANG2022` · **Catalogue entry:** [N05](../../METHODS.md#n05) ·
> **Source:** Wang, Sun, Liu, Liu & Shang (2022), <https://doi.org/10.1177/10567895221091308> ·
> **Implementation:** [`cp_methods/wang2022.py`](../../src/multifat/models/cp_methods/wang2022.py)

## Overview

- **Type and family:** stress-based critical-plane criterion and life model.
- **Idea:** on the plane of maximum shear stress amplitude $C_a$, the shear amplitude and a
  weighted normal stress amplitude are combined into an equivalent shear stress $t_{eq,a}$. Life
  then follows from the material's **torsional** S–N curve.
- **Mean stress:** the weight contains a maximum equivalent normal stress of SWT form,
  $\sqrt{N_{\max}N_a}$, meant to account for mean stress.
- **Proposed for:** high-cycle fatigue under proportional and out-of-phase tension (or bending)
  and torsion: aluminium alloys, 30CrMnSiA and SM45C steels.
- **Status:** implemented for **fully reversed loading only**, by maintainer decision (see
  [Repository choices](#repository-implementation)). Eqs. (10), (15) and (18)–(24) were checked on
  the paper. The predicted lives of Tables 4 and 5 are reproduced exactly.

## Original formulation

Wang et al. (2022, Eqs. 18–19):

```math
t_{eq,a} = \sqrt{C_a^2 + k\left(1 - \frac{N^*_{\max}}{2\sigma_y}\right)^2 N_a^2} \le \lambda = t_{-1},
\qquad N^*_{\max} = \sqrt{N_{\max}N_a},\quad N_{\max} = N_a + N_m,
```

```math
k = \frac{4t_{-1}^2 - f_{-1}^2}{f_{-1}^2\left(1 - \dfrac{f_{-1}}{4\sigma_y}\right)^2},
```

where:

- $C_a$ is the shear stress amplitude on the plane: the major semi-axis of the shear path, by the
  minimum circumscribed ellipse method (Eq. 10);
- $N_a$ and $N_m$ are the normal stress amplitude and mean on the plane;
- $\sigma_y$ is the yield strength.

$k$ makes both fully reversed limits satisfy the criterion. $k > 0$ requires $t_{-1}/f_{-1} > 0.5$
(Eq. 21).

**Life (Eqs. 22–24).** $t_{eq,a}$ equals the torsional S–N curve, either
$\tau_f'(2N_f)^{b_1}$ (Basquin) or $t_{-1}/(1 - \kappa N_f^{b_2})$ (three-parameter form).

## Repository implementation

$\mathrm{DP} = t_{eq,a}$ (MPa), evaluated on the plane of maximum $C_a$:

- $C_a$ is half the longest chord of the shear path (Eq. 10);
- ties of the maximum $C_a$ are resolved towards the larger $t_{eq,a}$;
- the master curve is fitted on the **pure torsion R = -1 failures**
  (`design_loading = "torsion"`), as the torsional S–N curve of Eq. (24).

**Repository choices**

- *Mean stress (maintainer decision).*
  - **The finding.** With Eq. (18) as printed, a tensile mean normal stress *raises*
    $N^*_{\max}$ and so *lowers* $t_{eq,a}$, i.e. it predicts a longer life. This contradicts the
    paper's own text and the experimental evidence.
  - **Why the printed form is kept.** It reproduces exactly every predicted life of Tables 4–5,
    which have no mean stress. A "+" sign reproduces none of them, and the SM45C mean-stress lives
    of Table 7 are reproduced by neither.
  - **What the repository does.** Eq. (18) is implemented as printed. A load that puts a mean
    normal stress on the critical plane is **out of scope** (`LoadingScopeError`): the model is
    skipped for that material, with the reason in the run report.
- *Life law.* A single power law replaces the three-parameter form of Eq. (23).
- *No `_ext` variant.* The model is registered at case level because the scope check needs the
  critical plane.

## Material constants and inputs

| Symbol | Python / workbook name | Unit | Source | Required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $f_{-1}$ | `Sigm1` | MPa | workbook | yes |
| $t_{-1}$ | `Taum1` | MPa | workbook | yes ($t_{-1}/f_{-1} > 0.5$) |
| $\sigma_y$ | `Sy` | MPa | workbook | yes |
| $\boldsymbol\sigma(t)$ | `S_mean` (no normal mean on the critical plane), `S_sin`, `S_cos` | MPa | Dataset sheet | yes |

## Calibration

1. **Original procedure.** $k$ follows from $f_{-1}$, $t_{-1}$ and $\sigma_y$ (Eq. 19). The life
   curve is the torsional S–N curve of the material, fitted on torsion tests.
2. **Repository.** The same $k$. The master curve $A N_f^b$ is fitted on the workbook's fully
   reversed torsion failures, which are reported as design rows (`Flag = 0`). Tension and
   multiaxial tests are predictions. A workbook without such torsion failures skips the model.

## Worked example

LY12CZ aluminium alloy, constants of the paper's Table 3.

```python
import numpy as np

from multifat.models.cp_methods.wang2022 import wang_k
from multifat.models.errors import LoadingScopeError
from multifat.models.registry import get_models
from multifat.planes.sampling import build_R_list

wang = get_models(["WANG2022"])[0]
planes = build_R_list(n1=20, n2=40)
zero = np.zeros((3, 3))
# LY12CZ aluminium alloy (paper's Table 3): limits, yield strength, torsional Basquin constants
params = {"Sigm1": 168.73, "Taum1": 119.62, "Sy": 400.0}
tau_f, b1 = 602.8, -0.1115


def t_eq(sigma_a, tau_a, delta_deg, sigma_m=0.0):
    """t_eq,a for sigma_x = sigma_m + sigma_a sin wt, tau_xy = tau_a sin(wt - delta)."""
    d = np.radians(delta_deg)
    S_mean = np.diag([sigma_m, 0.0, 0.0])
    S_sin = np.array([[sigma_a, tau_a * np.cos(d), 0.0], [tau_a * np.cos(d), 0.0, 0.0], [0, 0, 0]])
    S_cos = np.array([[0.0, -tau_a * np.sin(d), 0.0], [-tau_a * np.sin(d), 0.0, 0.0], [0, 0, 0]])
    result = wang.evaluate_case(
        S0=S_mean + S_sin, S1=S_mean - S_sin, E0=zero, E1=zero, params=params, R_list=planes,
        harmonics={"S_mean": S_mean, "S_sin": S_sin, "S_cos": S_cos, "E_sin": zero, "E_cos": zero},
    )  # fmt: skip
    return result.values[""]


print(f"k = {wang_k(168.73, 119.62, 400.0):.4f} (Eq. 19)")
for row, (s, t, d, n_pro) in {1: (126.491, 91.571, 0, 502272), 10: (158.114, 139.111, 90, 10284)}.items():
    life = 0.5 * (t_eq(s, t, d) / tau_f) ** (1 / b1)  # Eq. (22)-(24): t_eq = tau'_f (2 N)^b1
    print(f"Table 4 row {row:2d}: t_eq,a = {t_eq(s, t, d):6.1f} MPa, N = {life:7.0f} (paper {n_pro})")

try:
    t_eq(126.491, 91.571, 0, sigma_m=50.0)
except LoadingScopeError as err:
    print("with a mean normal stress:", str(err).split(";")[0])
```

Output:

```text
k = 1.2627 (Eq. 19)
Table 4 row  1: t_eq,a =  129.1 MPa, N =  502273 (paper 502272)
Table 4 row 10: t_eq,a =  199.2 MPa, N =   10284 (paper 10284)
with a mean normal stress: WANG2022: mean normal stress on the critical plane
```

The two lives match the paper's Table 4 to the cycle. With a 50 MPa mean stress, the case is out of
scope.

**On a supplied workbook:**

```bash
uv run multifat-pipeline --material 42CrMo4_Santus --models WANG2022 --results-dir Results/current/guides
```

On 42CrMo4_Santus the torsion S–N curve is flat ($k = 19.7 > 15$), so only damage-parameter errors are
reported. Workbooks with mean stresses (e.g. Al7075_T6) skip the model.

## Comparison capabilities and outputs

- **Life:** master curve (`Nf_expected`) fitted on the torsion failures.
- **DP comparison:** supported; $t_{eq,a}$ is homogeneous of degree 1 in the load.
- **Design set:** it differs from most methods (torsion instead of tension). Comparisons restricted
  to common observations should use `--flag_filter other`.
- No `_ext` variant.

## Verification and limitations

[`tests/methods/test_wang2022.py`](../../tests/methods/test_wang2022.py) checks Tables 3–7:

- **Tables 4 and 5.** Every predicted life is reproduced within 1 %: 15 LY12CZ and 7 30CrMnSiA
  tests. The `rss` amplitude misses the 90° rows by factors of 1.4–3.2.
- **Table 6 (6082-T6).** Rows 1–5 are reproduced. Rows 6–11, at phases of 94° and 125°, are strict
  expected failures.
- **Table 7 (SM45C).** Rows 1, 2 and 4 are reproduced. Rows 3 and 5–9, at 90°, are strict
  expected failures. Neither a surface-plane restriction nor the maximum $t_{eq,a}$ over planes
  reproduces them all; unresolved. Rows 10–18 carry a mean stress and raise `LoadingScopeError`.
- **Other checks:** Eq. (19) at both limits, and the printed sign behaviour that motivates the
  scope rule.

Not reproduced: the fatigue-limit validation (Fig. 3). Table 1 gives no yield strength for its nine
materials, and it lists "mild steel" with the limits of 42CrMo4.

Limitations:

- fully reversed loading only;
- requires $\sigma_y$ and $t_{-1}/f_{-1} > 0.5$;
- a power-law master curve instead of the three-parameter law.
