# Suggested implementation roadmap

These are research recommendations, not formally agreed WG4.3 commitments. No new fatigue law is implemented by this documentation change. Preserve the audit baseline and select one original formulation per PR.

## Existing-code evidence and restoration

First resolve LIU2021 Eq. (27), then MGSE_ZHU, MKBM, LI and scalar MSWT scope. Their code is active but completeness remains unestablished. Restore CARSPA plane orientation, Dang Van simultaneous shakedown/shear–hydrostatic history, the bounded OTT reduction/full-model distinction, physically paired SWTD directions and the actual ZHU_EDP work/path model in separate tasks. Prepared briefs specify evidence and acceptance criteria.

## Why the roadmap is shorter than the catalogue

This roadmap selects **22 of 97 missing method/scoped candidates** for an initial programme. **75 remain outside the sequence**: 66 with preliminary A/B feasibility and 9 requiring major extensions. They remain contribution opportunities, including medium/low-priority candidates. The five existing repairs and five existing-source reviews above form separate tracks.

Selection balances recognized CA fatigue relevance, additional scientific capability, methodological diversity, reuse of current utilities, reference/benchmark leads, material data and shared prerequisites. Numerical simplicity is not the sole criterion: volumetric, mesoscopic and probabilistic extensions are deliberately retained. Order is advisory and may change when original papers/data establish better feasibility.

The earlier research shortlist had 23 entries. G14 (Glinka–Shen–Plumtree) was subsequently held for overlap review against GSE/GSA, leaving 22 in this sequence; it remains in the full catalogue and is not asserted equivalent.

No candidate is rejected just because it is absent here. Deferred rows have explicit requirements in the [complete per-record reconciliation](docs/COUNT_RECONCILIATION.md) and [contribution index](docs/METHOD_INDEX.md). For work outside the sequence, choose its permanent ID, check source identity and related criteria, then open a scoped proposal using the method template. A different sequence is welcome when justified by a source-faithful formulation and available validation data.

Full VA/random-only workflows are deferred from CA priorities. Bundles and uncertain identities need specification first; application/comparison papers are not automatically new laws. Those dispositions do not remove their source records from the catalogue.

## FABER WG4.3 phase and external-code prerequisites

This programme supports a review article in preparation. Near-term implementation targets plain/smooth CA fatigue, P/NP only where the chosen law supports it. Gradient, volume, notch, structural and VA/random targets below are retained as potential future/reference tracks, not current capabilities or formal commitments. Each row now states that boundary.

Before writing a missing law from scratch, inspect [external sources](docs/EXTERNAL_IMPLEMENTATIONS.md): openfat may help Crossland/Sines; FLife provides a COIN-LiWI amplitude kernel; ASU shares G35 model/case scripts, but no reuse grant was identified. Source availability changes preparation tasks, not scientific validation or priority automatically. FS-Sol/FI-Sol can help benchmark existing proportional branches. ECP remains future geometry work. No licenses or third-party code are adopted here.

## Reusable prerequisites

| Component | Existing reuse | Beneficiaries / boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Physical sampled repeating cycle | Mean/sine/cosine tensors; direct model interface | Periodic nonsinusoidal paths, energy integration and simultaneous invariant evaluation. No arbitrary-cycle importer currently exists. |
| Explicit source-specific amplitude rules | BP major-axis helpers and legacy CP RSS convention | Papuga, Papadopoulos, invariant/path methods; RSS and MCC must not be silently interchanged. |
| Orientation quadrature and convergence | BP sphere grid and projections | PI/PIN, Robert–Fogue–Bahuaud, Liu–Zenner; source-specific weights required. |
| Native fatigue resistance/life result | Current empirical fitting only where mathematically appropriate | MDC, MWCM, MMCCM, Morel and energy-life laws; do not replace native equations with one generic fit. |
| Prescribed stabilized strain response | Tensor projection and plane search | Energy/strain criteria; elastic workbook strains do not reproduce plastic work or NP hardening. |
| Gradient/spatial fields with units and weights | Direct evaluator can be extended; no mesh/volume processor | G29 bounded gradient input first; volumetric/nonlocal methods subsequently. |
| Probability calibration with run-outs | Raw workbook provenance; processed importer filters run-outs | Probability/weakest-link criteria; no claim of probabilistic validity from current point fits. |

The full catalogue retains every other A/B candidate and wider C workstream. Bibliographic title/DOI identity does not establish reproducible equations. Conditional candidates and unresolved overlaps need source review before assignment. Future benchmarks should provide units, cyclic conventions, material coefficients, actual paths and intermediate results.
